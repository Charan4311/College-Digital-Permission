## 6. How Year & Branch Isolation Works

1. **Student Login**: Any student logs in using their **Roll Number** as both username and password. The system identifies their **Year** (2, 3, or 4) and **Branch** (CSM, CAI, CSD, AIDS, or CSC).
2. **First Stage (CTPO Approval)**: The request lands **only** in their branch's dedicated Year CTPO queue:
   - 2nd Year CSM ➔ `2ktcsm`
   - 3rd Year CAI ➔ `3ktcai`
   - 4th Year AIDS ➔ `4ktaids`
   *(Other CTPOs cannot see or approve requests outside their assigned Year and Branch).*
3. **Second Stage (HOD Approval)**: Once approved by the CTPO, the request routes directly to their corresponding Year HOD:
   - 2nd Year ➔ `2kthod`
   - 3rd Year ➔ `3kthod`
   - 4th Year ➔ `4kthod`
4. **Third Stage (Hostel In-charge)**: If the student selected **Hosteler**, the request routes to `hostel_incharge`. If **Day Scholar**, it bypasses the warden and issues the gate pass immediately.
5. **Final Gate Checkout**: Security logs in as `security` and scans the QR code at the main campus gate.

## 7. Multi-Feature Permission & Clearance Workflows

| Feature | Student Input Fields | Approval Workflow Chain | Final Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Campus Out-Pass** | Reason, Out Date & Time, Return Date & Time, Student Category (`DAY_SCHOLAR` / `HOSTELER`) | • **Day Scholar**: Student ➔ CTPO ➔ HOD ➔ Issued<br>• **Hosteler**: Student ➔ CTPO ➔ HOD ➔ Hostel In-charge ➔ Issued | Single-use QR Gate Pass scanned by Security at campus exit |
| **2. Mess Fee Clearance** | Clearance Reason, Start Date, End Date, Mess Amount (₹), Payment Status (`Paid`/`Partially Paid`/`Not Paid`), Optional Receipt Upload | Student ➔ Branch CTPO ➔ Year HOD ➔ Cleared | Official printable **Mess Fee Clearance Certificate** with digital seals |
| **3. Internship Permission** | Company Name, Location, Role, Mode (`Offline`/`Online`/`Hybrid`), Start Date, End Date, Optional Offer Letter Upload | Student ➔ Branch CTPO ➔ Year HOD ➔ Placement Officer (`placement_officer`) ➔ Approved | Official printable **Internship Permission Letter** with triple authorization |
| **4. Library Permission** | Access Purpose / Details, Access / Clearance Date, Auto-filled Roll No & Name | Student ➔ Branch CTPO ➔ Approved *(Fast-Track 1-Stage)* | Official printable **Library Clearance & Access Pass** with CTPO digital seal |

### Rejection & Resubmission Cycle
- When any approver (CTPO, HOD, Hostel In-charge, Placement Officer) rejects a request, an **official mandatory remark** must be supplied.
- The student's dashboard flags the rejection in red with the reviewer's remarks clearly displayed.
- The student can click **"Edit & Resubmit Request"**, rectify dates/amounts or attach missing proof, and resubmit it back to the CTPO for a fresh review cycle (tracked by `Resubmitted #N`).
